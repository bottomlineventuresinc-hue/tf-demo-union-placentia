Asked every week

Straight answers about mess, timing, and money.

Will there be mess?

Yes. Outdoor digs stay marked, plant protection goes up when we work near beds, and there is a sweep-down at the end of every working day.

Will it run over?

Most jobs finish on the written schedule. Hidden damage, inspection queues, and made-to-order delays are the honest exceptions. You hear from your site lead within 24 hours with the revised date in writing.

How do payments work?

Staged in the contract: 10% to book, 30% on start, 30% at mid-point, 25% at finish work, and the final 5% only after the punch list is cleared at sign-off.

Do we have to move out?

Usually not. Landscaping and hardscape work stays outdoors. Indoor access is only needed if we stage tools through a garage you approve.
